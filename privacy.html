Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

content_manager ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single individual, its developer, to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When the developer logs in with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and an access token, and uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload video files to the account's TikTok inbox.

How data is used and stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer, in a file readable only by the developer.
  • Videos are read from the developer's local disk and sent directly to TikTok. The app keeps no copy.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • The app has no servers, database or tracking of its own.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file. Access can also be revoked from TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
